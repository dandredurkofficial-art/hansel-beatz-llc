Our story

A label shaped by a love for music.

Hansel Beatz LLC is an independent record label built on the belief that original music deserves thoughtful support and a platform to reach listeners.

Our work is centred on creative collaboration, artist development, and bringing music from the studio to audiences. We value individuality, consistency, and long-term creative growth.

This website is the home of our label identity, artist information, releases, and company updates.

01 / Mission

Support creativity

To support artists and distinctive music through collaboration, care, and a commitment to creative quality.

02 / Vision

Reach new listeners

To build a respected independent label with a growing catalogue and connections across music communities.

03 / Values

Integrity & originality

We value honesty, artistic identity, professionalism, collaboration, and respect for the work.
